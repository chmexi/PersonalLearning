## Variant: Obsidian Minimalist

### Design stance
Minimalist dark theme inspired by Obsidian. Content is the focus — every element earns its place.

### Key choices
- **Theme**: Dark mode (#1E1E2E background, Catppuccin palette)
- **Typography**: System fonts, low contrast for secondary info
- **Layout**: Simple list, no card backgrounds, thin dividers
- **Interaction**: Tap exercise to open; checkmark on completion
- **Navigation**: Bottom tab bar (训练/统计/设置)

### Trade-offs
- **Strong at**: Clean, focused feel; Obsidian users feel at home
- **Weak at**: Less visual hierarchy between exercises; might feel too spartan

### Best for
Users who want a distraction-free writing/training environment
